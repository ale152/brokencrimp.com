Map your beta.
Never forget a move.

Broken Crimp is a notebook for sport, trad and boulder climbers. Put the holds on a photo of the wall, set the moves in order, and play the sequence back at home or at the crag.

Get it on Google Play Open it in your browser

Free, with ads. No account, no sign-up. In English and Italian.

Draw the climb

Place holds on a photo of the real wall, from crimps and slopers to bolts and cams. Record which hand or foot goes where, and in what order.

Works at the crag

No signal, no problem. Everything works offline, and your climbs, photos and voice notes stay on your device. We run no servers.

Share it

Send a climb to a friend as a file their app opens, or export a PDF, a video of the sequence, or a plain list of the moves.
