Besides holds, the **Holds** tab has two more things you can add to the wall: lines that split a climb into sections, and words written on the rock. Both show on every beta of the climb.

## 1. Mark a section

To split a long climb into parts, choose **Add section divider…** in the {% include ui.html name="menu" %} menu on the **Holds** tab. Drag the line into place, press **Done**, and give the section a name if you want one, like the crux or the rest.

## 2. Write on the wall

**Add text…** in the same menu writes words directly on the wall, like *start sitting* or the way down. Type them and press **Add**. The text is then selected the way a hold is: drag a corner {% include ui.html button="corner" %} to resize or turn it, and the capsule {% include ui.html button="move" %} to move it. The buttons edit the words {% include ui.html button="edit" %}, change the colour {% include ui.html button="palette" %}, duplicate it {% include ui.html button="duplicate" %} and remove it {% include ui.html button="remove" %}.

## 3. See them, or hide them

Sections and words show on the **Beta maps** tab, but they can only be changed from the **Holds** tab. To hide them, untick **Section dividers** or **Text** in **Layers**. [The Layers guide](/guides/change-what-you-see-with-layers/) has the rest.
