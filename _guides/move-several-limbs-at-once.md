title: "Move several limbs at once"
description: "Set a double dyno or a hand and foot going together as one move."
---
Some moves aren't one limb at a time. Both hands leave for a double dyno, or a hand and a foot go together. Put them on one move and they travel at the same time when you press play {% include ui.html button="play" %}.

## 1. Choose the limbs

On the **Beta maps** tab, long-press a limb on the dial. It lights up, and a banner at the bottom of the wall lists it. Tap each limb that goes with it, and the banner adds them. To back out, press **Cancel** on the banner.

## 2. Place them

The banner says which limb is next. Tap the wall for each one. They end up on a single move with one number, and the bar under the wall shows them side by side. Play the beta, or step onto that move, and they travel together.

## 3. Separate them

A move with several limbs has an extra button on the bar, an arrow that splits in two {% include ui.html name="split" %}. Press it and choose **Separate**. Each limb becomes its own move, one after another.

<div class="shots">

</div>

## 4. Join two moves you already placed

Joining works from the later move: you tell it to go at the same time as the move just before it. The earlier move stays as it is, and the later one joins it.

Open the [advanced editor](/guides/rearrange-a-beta/) and tap the second of the two moves. Under **Same time**, tick **Move with previous**. The line below it says which move it joins. The two then sit in one bracket in the list.

## Your starting position

The first hands and feet you place join into one move by themselves, which becomes your starting position. Separate it as in step 3 if you need to.

To place every limb as its own move from the start, press the gear {% include ui.html name="gear" %} on the climb list to open **Settings**, and turn off **Automatic starting position** under **Building betas**.
