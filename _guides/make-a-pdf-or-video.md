Take a beta out of the app, to print or to send to someone who does not have it. Each of these covers the beta you have open, so switch to the right one first.

Open the climb and choose **Share…** in the {% include ui.html name="menu" %} menu.

## A PDF

Choose **PDF** for a deck with an overview of the climb and then one page per move. Swipe across to read through it before you make it. Press **Share as PDF** to hand it to another app, or to your printer.

Afterwards the page offers **Share** again and, on Android, **Save to device** to keep a copy.

## A video

Choose **Video**, and set up the clip:

* **Shape**: **Vertical** for a phone or a story, or **Horizontal**.
* **View**: **Follow climber** moves with the climber, and **Whole climb** keeps the whole route in the frame.
* **Trim**: drag the two handles to keep only part of the beta, like the crux.
* **Speed** and **Quality**: the line underneath shows the resulting length and size.

Press **Create video**. It takes a moment, and at the end the page offers the same two buttons as the PDF. In a browser, keep the page in front while it works.

Each frame names the move underneath the climber, so the clip makes sense with no explanation.

## Plain text

Choose **Text** for a list of the moves to paste into a message or a note, with **Copy** and **Share** buttons. Moves on a hold name that hold. Moves on the bare photo use arrows to show which way the hand or foot goes.
