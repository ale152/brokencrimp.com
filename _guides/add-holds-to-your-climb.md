order: 2
title: "Add holds to your climb"
description: "Name what you are holding, attach a close-up or a voice note to it, and keep the moves you already set."
---
A climb mapped on a photo needs no holds at all. Add a hold when you want to name what you are holding, like a crimp or a sloper, or to attach a close-up or a voice note to it. The moves you already set go onto the holds you add. There are [37 types of hold](/glossary/holds/) to choose from.

Indoors, the holds usually show up well enough in the photo. Outdoors they can be hard to pick out from the rock, and placing holds over the photo helps the line stand out.

## 1. Find your moves on the Holds tab

If you have already mapped a beta on the photo, open the **Holds** tab. Each small dot marks a spot where a hand or foot goes in one of your moves, so you can see where each hold belongs. A dot goes away once you place a hold on it.

Dots from the beta you have open are labelled: *4 · LH* is move 4, the left hand. Dots from your other betas on the climb show too, without a label. If you haven't mapped a beta yet, there are no dots, and you can place holds anywhere.

## 2. Pick a hold

The drawer at the bottom of the **Holds** tab holds your recent holds. Drag it up to see the rest under **Holds**, **Feet**, **Features** and **Protection**.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="drawer" alt="The holds drawer half open over the wall photo, showing recent holds and the Holds category." %}
{% include shot.html src="catalogue" alt="The drawer pulled up, showing feet, features like tufa and arête, and protection like bolts and cams." %}
</div>

## 3. Place it on a move

Tap a hold in the drawer, then tap the wall on or close to a dot. The hold snaps onto the dot, and the moves there go onto the hold, in every beta on the climb.

Or tap the dot itself. Pick a type in **Place a hold here**, and the hold goes straight onto that spot.

<div class="shots">
{% include clip.html src="pickup" alt="A jug is tapped in the drawer and then tapped on the wall beside the dot labelled 4 LH. The view zooms in, the hold sits on the spot, and the dot has gone." %}
</div>

## 4. Adjust it

The hold you placed is selected. Around it:

* Drag a corner {% include ui.html button="corner" %} to resize or turn it.
* Drag the capsule {% include ui.html button="move" %} beside it to move it.
* The buttons on its other side edit it {% include ui.html button="edit" %}, attach photos and voice notes {% include ui.html button="media" %}, duplicate it {% include ui.html button="duplicate" %} and remove it {% include ui.html button="remove" %}.

The edit button {% include ui.html button="edit" %} opens a panel with a nickname, the type, **Size** and **Angle**. **Bring to front** and **Send to back** decide which hold is on top where two overlap.

To select a hold later, tap it.

## 5. Move or remove a hold that has moves on it

Small nudges just happen. If you drag the hold further, the app asks first and says how many moves use it. **Move** takes its moves along, and **Cancel** puts the hold back.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="far" alt="A dialog asking Move Jug with its moves?, saying 2 moves in 2 betas use this hold, with Cancel and Move." %}
</div>

Press the hold's bin {% include ui.html button="remove" %}, and the app asks what happens to its moves:

* **Keep the moves** leaves them on the wall where they are, as dots again.
* **Remove them too** removes them from every beta.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="remove" alt="A dialog asking Remove Jug?, offering Keep the moves or Remove them too." %}
</div>

## 6. Place several of the same hold

Long-press a hold in the drawer to keep placing it, one tap at a time. Press **Done** or Back to stop. The app numbers holds of the same type for you.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="repeat" alt="Three numbered crimps placed on the wall photo, with a banner saying Placing Crimp until you stop." %}
</div>

## 7. Add a hold under every move

To do them all at once, choose **Add holds under the moves** in the {% include ui.html name="menu" %} menu on the **Holds** tab. Each spot gets a plain hold: a foothold where only feet go, and a handhold everywhere else. Tap one to change its type or give it a name. **Undo** {% include ui.html name="undo" %} takes them all back.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="generic" alt="The wall photo with plain brown shapes on the spots that had dots, named Handhold 1, Handhold 2, Handhold 4 and Foothold 4." %}
</div>

## 8. If you remove the photo

Your moves need something under them once the rock has gone. Open **Layers**, choose **Edit photo…**, and press the bin {% include ui.html name="remove" %} at the top right. The app warns you that plain holds will take the photo's place under the moves.

The same plain holds appear when you [share the climb](/guides/share-a-climb/) without its wall photo.
