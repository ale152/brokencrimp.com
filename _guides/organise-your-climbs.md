description: "Find a climb, change its details and its picture, and delete the ones you are done with."
---
The climb list is the first thing you see in the app. Once it grows, these keep it manageable.

## 1. Search

Type in **Search climbs** at the top of the list. It looks through climb names, beta names, notes and hold names, and shows where it found each match. Press {% include ui.html name="close" %} to clear it.

{% include shot.html src="search" alt="The word thumb in the search box, with three climbs listed, each showing the note that contains the word." %}
</div>

## 2. Filter and sort

Press the button {% include ui.html name="filter" %} beside the search box. **Type** shows only the kinds you tick, and a red dot on the button reminds you a filter is on. **Sort by** orders the list by **Date modified** or **Date created**.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="filter" alt="The filter menu with Sport ticked and Date modified chosen, and only sport climbs in the list." %}
</div>

## 3. Change a climb's details

Press the {% include ui.html name="menu" %} on a climb and choose **Edit** to change its name, type, grade or location. **Edit details…** in the {% include ui.html name="menu" %} menu inside the climb opens the same thing.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="menu" alt="A climb's menu with Edit, Change thumbnail, Remove thumbnail, Share and Delete." %}
</div>

## 4. Choose its picture

**Change thumbnail** in the same menu sets what the card shows. Drag the wall to move it inside the frame, pinch to zoom, and press **Save**. **Remove thumbnail** takes the picture off the card, and **Show thumbnail** brings it back.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="thumbnail" alt="The Change thumbnail dialog, with a square frame over the wall photo." %}
</div>

## 5. Delete climbs

To delete one, press its {% include ui.html name="menu" %} and choose **Delete**. To delete several, long-press a climb, tap the others, then press the bin {% include ui.html name="remove" %} at the top right and confirm. {% include ui.html name="close" %} leaves the selection without deleting anything.

A deleted climb takes its betas and media with it, and this can't be undone, so [back up](/guides/back-up-and-move-climbs/) anything you may want again.
