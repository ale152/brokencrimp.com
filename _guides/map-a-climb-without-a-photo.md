title: "No photo? Map a climb on a blank wall"
description: "Build the wall from holds instead of a photo, then set your moves on them."
---
A photo of the wall is the quickest start, and a photo of a topo works too. When you have neither, like for a board at home or a climb you remember, build the wall from holds instead.

## 1. Create the climb

Create the climb the way you would [with a photo](/guides/map-your-first-climb/). On your first climb, a card offers **Add a wall photo** and **Start with holds**. After that, a new climb opens on the **Holds** tab with the drawer of holds already open.

## 2. Place the holds

Press **Start with holds** if the card is there. Tap a hold in the drawer, then tap the wall where it goes.

<div class="shots">
{% include clip.html src="first-hold" alt="Start with holds is pressed, the drawer opens, a jug is tapped in it and then tapped on the blank wall, where it appears selected." %}
</div>

Place the rest the same way, roughly where they are on the real wall. The climber is sized to match your holds, so space them about as far apart as they are on the rock. To place several of the same hold, long-press it in the drawer.

[Add holds to your climb](/guides/add-holds-to-your-climb/) has the rest: resizing and turning a hold, naming it, and attaching a photo or a voice note to it.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="holds" alt="A blank wall with a jug, three crimps and two footholds, and a hint pointing at the Beta maps tab." %}
</div>

## 3. Set your moves on the holds

Open the **Beta maps** tab. Tap a limb on the dial, then tap a hold, and the hand or foot lands on it. Everything from here works as in [your first climb](/guides/map-your-first-climb/).
