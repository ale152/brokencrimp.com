title: "Rearrange a beta in the advanced editor"
description: "See every move as a list, and reorder, change or remove them."
---
You add moves on the wall, one at a time. The advanced editor shows the whole beta as a list, which makes it the quicker place to change what is already there.

## 1. Open it

Press the gear {% include ui.html name="gear" %} on the move's bar and choose **Advanced editor**, or choose it in the {% include ui.html name="menu" %} menu. Every move has a row, and a bracket marks moves that happen together. The arrow {% include ui.html name="back" %} at the top left takes you back to the wall, which is also where new moves go.

## 2. Change the order

Drag the dots {% include ui.html name="drag" %} at the left of a row to move it up or down, and the numbers update. A bracketed group has one set of dots {% include ui.html name="drag" %} and moves as a block.

<div class="shots">
{% include clip.html src="reorder" alt="The dots on move 7 are dragged up the list to before move 5, and the moves renumber." %}
</div>

## 3. Change a move

Tap a row to open its panel. Everything about the move is here: the limb, whether it goes with the move before, the move's name, its [pin](/guides/pin-a-limb/), its tags and its note.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="panel" alt="A move's panel with Same time, Move, Pin, Tags, Pause and Note." %}
</div>

## 4. Set a pause

**Pause** in the same panel sets how long a move waits before the next one, from **None** to **As long as a rest**. The middle of the slider is the usual wait. Use it to hold on a hard position when you play the beta, or in a video of it. A move tagged **Rest** always waits the longest.

## 5. Remove several moves

Each row has its own bin {% include ui.html name="remove" %}. To remove several together, long-press a row, tap the others, then press the bin {% include ui.html name="remove" %} at the top right and confirm. {% include ui.html name="close" %} leaves the selection without removing anything.

**Undo** {% include ui.html name="undo" %} at the top of the page brings back what you removed.
