Send a climb, with all its betas, as a file someone opens in their own copy of the app. They can change it, add their own beta and send it back. To send something to a person without the app, [make a PDF or a video](/guides/make-a-pdf-or-video/) instead.

## 1. Open the share sheet

Open the climb and choose **Share…** in the {% include ui.html name="menu" %} menu.

The sheet offers four ways to share:

* **PDF**: a document with the whole climb, then one page per move, to print or keep on your phone at the crag.
* **Video**: the climber playing your moves, to send to anyone, even someone without the app.
* **Text**: a plain list of the moves, to paste into a message or a note.
* **Backup .beta file**: the climb itself, with all its betas, for someone to open and change in Broken Crimp.

[Make a PDF or a video](/guides/make-a-pdf-or-video/) covers the first three. The rest of this guide is about the .beta file.

## 2. Choose what goes in the file

Choose **Backup .beta file**, and open **Export options**.

**Wall photo** is ticked, because a climb is hard to read without its rock. It includes the full-size original, which makes the bigger part of the file: a single climb can be quite a few MB with its photo, and the same one just a few KB without it.

If the climb has them, **Hold photos** and **Voice notes** appear here too, unticked. Your voice notes stay with you unless you choose to send them.

## 3. Send it

Press **Export**, and your phone's own share menu opens. Send the file any way you like. Nothing goes through a Broken Crimp server, because there isn't one.

## 4. The other person opens it

The other person presses **Import .beta file**, the icon {% include ui.html name="import" %} at the top of the climb list, and picks the file. The app says what is in it before anything happens. **Import** adds it as a new climb and opens it.
