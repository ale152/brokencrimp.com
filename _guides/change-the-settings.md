Press the gear {% include ui.html name="gear" %} at the top of the climb list to open **Settings**.

## 1. Appearance and language

* **Theme** chooses **Follow system**, **Light** or **Dark**. The app starts light until you pick.
* **Language** chooses English or Italian, or **Follow system** to use your device's language. These are the only two for now, and more languages will follow.

## 2. Feedback and guidance

* **Vibration** turns on the small buzz some controls give when you use them.
* **Show instructions** turns the first-time guides on or off: the cards and bubbles that explain a screen the first time you meet it. Turning it back on shows them all again from the start.
* **Show tips** turns the tips on or off, short facts about the app that appear now and then. Turning it back on shows every tip again.
* **Show me a tip** shows a tip straight away. Once you have read them all, they start again.

## 3. Building betas

* **Automatic starting position** joins the first hands and feet you place on a new beta into one starting position, move 1. Turn it off to place every limb as its own move. [Move several limbs at once](/guides/move-several-limbs-at-once/) has more.
* **Automatically drop limbs** lets the climber let go of a hand or foot it has left behind and can no longer reach. Turn it off and every limb stays on its hold, stretching as far as it has to. [Pin a hand or foot](/guides/pin-a-limb/) does the same for one limb.

## 4. Your data

* **Back up all your climbs** saves every climb, beta, photo and voice note to one file.
* **Restore from a backup** reads a backup file, then replaces what you have or adds to it.

[Back up and move your climbs](/guides/back-up-and-move-climbs/) explains both, and which kind of restore to choose.
