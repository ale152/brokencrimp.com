See where your beta and a friend's differ, or decide between two ways of doing your own crux, on the same wall.

## 1. Add a second beta

Tap the beta's name at the top of the **Beta maps** tab and choose **Add a beta**, then set the moves. To change only a few moves of a beta you already have, choose **Duplicate beta** in the {% include ui.html name="menu" %} menu instead.

The same menu has **Rename beta**, so you can tell them apart by name, and **Delete beta** for one you no longer want. Deleting a beta leaves the climb and its holds alone.

</div>

## 2. Choose the two

Choose **Compare betas…** in the {% include ui.html name="menu" %} menu, tick two betas, and press **Compare**.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="pick" alt="The Compare betas dialog with Pete and Tom ticked, Pete marked blue and Tom orange." %}
</div>

## 3. Press play

The two climbers share the wall, each in its beta's colour. **Sync** is on from the start and lines them up by where they are on the wall, so the one ahead waits for the other. That is why the counters can differ, as in the clip below: move 4 of one beta sits level with move 3 of the other.

Press the **Sync** pill to turn it off and let each beta run at its own pace. Each climber also has its own slider, to move it by hand.

<div class="shots">
{% include clip.html src="play" alt="Two climbers in blue and orange play on the same wall, each counter moving as the one ahead waits for the other, until both reach move 8." %}
</div>

## 4. Split them apart

Where the two overlap too much to read, press the **Side by side** button {% include ui.html name="sidebyside" %} at the top right. Each climber gets its own half of the screen. The arrows button {% include ui.html name="swap" %} beside it swaps the two.

Press {% include ui.html name="close" %} to go back to your beta.
