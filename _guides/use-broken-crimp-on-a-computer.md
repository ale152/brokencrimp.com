The [web version](/app/) is Broken Crimp in a browser tab. It has the same screens as the Android app, so every other guide applies, and a big screen makes mapping a long climb easier. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

## 1. Check your browser

The web version runs in:

* Chrome or Microsoft Edge, version 119 or later
* Firefox 120 or later
* Safari 18.2 or later

These run on Windows, macOS, Linux and Chromebooks. On an older Mac whose Safari can't be updated, use Chrome or Firefox instead. On an Android phone, [the app from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brokencrimp.app) is the better choice, and an iPhone has [its own guide](/guides/iphone/).

If your browser is too old, the page says *This browser can't run Broken Crimp* instead of loading. Update the browser, or open the page in another one.

## 2. Open it

Open [the web version](/app/) and let it load. The first visit downloads the whole app, and a bar shows how far it has got. After that it opens straight away, even with no connection.

{% include shot.html src="loading" wide=true width="840" height="525" alt="The web version loading: the Broken Crimp icon and name, a bar partly filled, and the note that the first visit takes longer while the app downloads." %}

To open it like any other program, install it from the browser. In Chrome and Edge, press the install button at the right of the address bar. On a Mac, Safari has **Add to Dock** in its **File** menu. Installed this way it opens in its own window, with the climbs still kept in the browser.

## 3. Use a mouse and keyboard

Everything the guides say to tap, click. Drag with the mouse where they say to drag.

* **Zoom:** the scroll wheel zooms the wall in and out around the pointer. On a trackpad, pinch or slide two fingers.
* **Move around the wall:** drag it.
* **Long-press:** press and hold the mouse button.
* **Confirm a dialog:** press Enter.
* **Back:** the browser's back button goes back a page, from a climb to the list. To close a panel or leave a comparison, use its own {% include ui.html name="close" %} or **Done**.

**Full screen** in **Layers** fills the screen with the wall. Press Esc or the same switch to leave it.

## 4. Only one tab at a time

Your climbs open in one tab at a time, so two tabs never save over each other. If Broken Crimp is already open somewhere else, a new tab says *Broken Crimp is open in another tab*. Press **Use here** to move to the new tab, and the other one lets go of your climbs.

## 5. What works differently

* **Photos:** on a computer, pick the wall photo or a hold photo from your files. A phone's browser also offers its camera. Photos saved as HEIC, which many phones use by default, can't be opened. Pick a JPEG or PNG instead, or set the phone's camera to save JPEG. On an iPhone this setting is called *Most Compatible*.
* **Voice notes:** the browser asks for the microphone the first time you record.
* **Media:** links, notes and photos work. **Video from device** is on Android only, so add a link to the video instead.
* **Sharing:** a .beta file, a PDF or a video arrives as a download. Send it on from there.
* **Videos:** the browser makes the video, and an older browser may not be able to. If **Video** isn't in the share options, update the browser.
* **Long exports:** a big PDF or a long video can take minutes. Before it starts, the app says roughly how long. Keep the page open and in front until it finishes, because a browser slows down a tab you aren't looking at.

## 6. Know where your climbs are kept

Your climbs, photos and voice notes are saved in the browser, on that computer, and nowhere else. Nothing is sent to a server, because there isn't one. This has some consequences:

* **Each browser has its own climbs.** Chrome can't see what you made in Firefox, and a second browser profile starts empty.
* **They aren't on your phone.** The Android app and the web version keep separate climbs.
* **A private or incognito window keeps nothing.** Everything goes when the window closes.
* **Clearing your browsing data can delete them.** Clearing cookies and site data for all sites, or for brokencrimp.com, removes your climbs too.
* **A browser can clear them by itself.** The app asks the browser to keep its data, and most do. Safari can still delete a website's data after a week without a visit, and a browser short of disk space can clear sites to make room.
* **An installed copy may have its own climbs.** On a Mac, Safari's Add to Dock version can start without the climbs saved in Safari. Move them across with a backup.

## 7. Back up

A backup is the one copy that doesn't depend on the browser. In **Settings**, choose **Back up all your climbs**, and keep the file somewhere other than that computer. Do it again after a session of mapping.

The same file moves your climbs:

* **from your phone to the computer:** make a backup in the Android app and restore it in the web version;
* **back again:** make a backup in the web version and restore it on the phone;
* **to another browser or computer:** restore the backup there.

The copies don't stay in sync. [Back up and move your climbs](/guides/back-up-and-move-climbs/) explains the two ways to restore and which one to choose.
