---
order: 5
title: "Pin a hand or foot"
description: "Keep a limb on its hold when the climber moves away from it."
---
When the climber moves too far from a hold, the app drops the hand or foot on it, and the limb hangs free. Keeping it on the hold would stretch the arm or leg longer than a real one, so most of the time dropping it is right.

The climber is only an approximation of a body, though, and a real one often reaches further than the figure suggests: a long reach, a wide bridge, a heel hook far out to the side. When the limb really does stay on, pin it to keep it on its hold anyway.

A pinned limb stretches as far as it has to, with no limit, so it can look much longer than the rest of the figure.

## 1. Pin the limb

Go to the move that placed the limb, with the arrows {% include ui.html button="previous" %} {% include ui.html button="next" %} or the slider. Press the gear {% include ui.html name="gear" %} on the move's bar, choose **Pin**, and tick **This limb is pinned**. On a move where [several limbs go at the same time](/guides/move-several-limbs-at-once/), the dialog lists each of them.

## 2. Check it on the wall

A small pin {% include ui.html name="pin" %} appears beside the limb's label on the **Beta maps** tab. The limb stays on its hold until you place it again or it lets go.

<div class="shots">
{% include shot.html src="mark" wide=true width="600" height="600" alt="A close-up of the left hand's label on the wall, with a small grey pin beside it." %}
</div>

To undo it, open the same dialog and untick the box.

## Turn dropping off altogether

To keep every limb on its hold on all your climbs, press the gear {% include ui.html name="gear" %} on the climb list to open **Settings** and turn off **Automatically drop limbs**. Every limb then stretches as far as it has to, on every move.
