A wall full of labels can be hard to read. **Layers** decides what is drawn, and hiding something never changes the climb.

## 1. Open the panel

Press the button {% include ui.html button="layers" %} at the top right of the wall, on either tab. Its rows depend on the climb and the tab: a climb with no holds has no **Holds** row, and a climb with section dividers or text on the wall has a row for each of those.

## 2. Fade what gets in the way

**Holds**, **Body** and **Background** are sliders. Drag one left to fade that layer, and all the way to hide it. Fading the photo makes the holds and the climber easier to pick out.

<div class="shots">

## 3. Choose the words on the wall

**Hold names** decides which holds are labelled on the wall:

* **Off** hides every name and leaves the rock clear.
* **Nicknames** shows only the names you gave holds yourself, like *the pinch* or *good jug*. It appears once at least one hold has a nickname.
* **All** labels every hold, with its nickname if it has one and otherwise its type and number, like *Crimp 2*.

On the **Beta maps** tab, **Moves** decides what is written beside the climber's hands and feet:

* **Off** shows no labels, only the figure.
* **Numbers** gives each hand and foot a small label with the limb and its move number, like *LH 3*.
* **Numbers + notes** also writes each move's note and tags beside its label.

Fewer words make the climb easier to read on a small screen. More help when you are learning the sequence.

## 4. Decide whether the wall follows the climber

**Follow current move** is on the **Beta maps** tab and starts ticked, so the wall moves to keep the current move in sight when you press play {% include ui.html button="play" %}. Untick it to keep the wall still.

## 5. Go full screen

Tick **Full screen** to hide the app's title bar and tabs, and give the wall that space. Close the panel to see it. To leave, press Back, or untick the same box.

## 6. Fix the wall photo later

**Edit photo…** reopens the editor you [framed the photo in](/guides/map-your-first-climb/). Crop it, straighten it with **Correct the perspective**, or turn it with **Rotate**, then press **Save**. Your holds and moves go along with the rock, and **Undo** {% include ui.html name="undo" %} on the wall puts everything back.

**Replace** swaps in a new picture, and your holds and moves stay where they are. To line the new picture up with them, press **Move**. Drag the photo into place, use its corners {% include ui.html button="corner" %} to resize or turn it, and press **Done**. Only the photo moves.
