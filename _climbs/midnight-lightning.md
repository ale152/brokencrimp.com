Midnight Lightning is a boulder problem on the Columbia Boulder, in the middle of Camp 4 in Yosemite Valley. Ron Kauk climbed it in May 1978, and after John Bachar's repeat the same month it went five years without another ascent. It is 7.6 m high, steep to a lip almost 5 m up and a slab above, and a lightning bolt drawn in chalk marks the start.

## History

John "Yabo" Yablonski spotted the line early in 1978. The name comes from a Jimi Hendrix song. Kauk worked on it for about two months and made the first ascent in May 1978. Bachar made the second soon after and drew a lightning bolt in chalk beside the start.

The third ascent took five years: Skip Guerin, in 1983. Lynn Hill made the first ascent by a woman in 1998, and Lisa Rands followed in 2001. Adam Ondra flashed it in October 2018.

A small hold near the mantel broke soon after the first ascent, which made the finish harder. The chalk bolt has been redrawn many times. In April 2013 James Lucas scrubbed it off, and it was back within days.

## The climb

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| The start | Polished crimps and a first hard move to an undercling |
| The jump | From a crimp about 3 m up to the lightning bolt hold, the feet swinging off |
| The lip | A swing right, then a move to the lip under the slab |
| The mantel | A press over a high, slippery foot, almost 5 m off the ground |
| The slab | A good edge, then easy climbing to the top |

Tall climbers can reach the lightning bolt hold without jumping. The mantel is the famous part: Kauk fell from it before his first ascent, and Lynn Hill practised it on a rope.

## The beta

This is the usual sequence, put together from written accounts of several ascents. The file is a drawing: the holds are placed from those descriptions and are not measured from the rock. The feet low down, the order of the hands at the lip and the slab are a best guess.

1. **The start.** Stand on the footholds with both hands on the polished [crimps](/glossary/holds/#crimp). Fire the right hand up to an [undercling](/glossary/holds/#undercling) crimp. Many tries end on this move.
2. **The set-up.** Move the left hand up, then [bump](/glossary/moves/#bump) it to a crimp about 3 m off the ground and set the feet.
3. **The jump.** [Jump](/glossary/moves/#dyno) with the right hand to the lightning bolt hold. The feet come off and the body swings. [Match](/glossary/moves/#match) the hold with the left hand.
4. **The lip.** Throw the body right and walk the feet through to a smear on the right. Reach the lip with the right hand, then the left, and put the right foot high on a slippery smear. You are hanging under the lip on long arms.
5. **The mantel.** Push up on both hands until the shoulders are over the lip, elbows out, the left foot off the wall, then over onto the right foot. This is the [mantle](/glossary/moves/#mantle) the problem is known for, and the body position has to be exact.
6. **The slab.** Press out and reach a good edge on the slab with the left hand. Bring the left foot onto the lip and stand up, the right hand leaving the lip, then walk to the top.
