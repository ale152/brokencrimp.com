# What's new in Broken Crimp
{:.no_toc}

## Release 8

* Compare two betas on the same wall
* Start a climb from a photo and map your moves straight onto it, no holds needed
* A hand or foot can now let go of a hold
* Add text to the wall
* Editing the wall photo keeps your holds and moves on the rock
* Save a finished video or PDF to your device
* Many smaller fixes and improvements
