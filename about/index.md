# About Broken Crimp

Broken Crimp is a notebook for sport, trad and boulder climbers. You set the moves of a climb on a photo of the wall, in order, and play the sequence back at home or at the crag.

It never tells you how to climb. The beta is yours to work out, or a friend's to send you.

It is made by an independent developer. Your climbs, photos and voice notes stay on your device, because there are no servers to send them to. The app is free, and a few ads keep it that way.
