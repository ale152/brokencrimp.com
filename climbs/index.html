Famous climbs

The moves of some of the world's best known climbs, animated move by move and open to anyone to read. Every page lists the sources it was put together from.

To see a climb move by move, download its .beta file from its page and open it in Broken Crimp with Import .beta file, the icon {% include ui.html name="import" %} at the top of the climb list. Then play it, step through it, or change it to fit your own beta. Silence, Burden of Dreams and DNA already come with the app. How to import a climb shows each step.
